## Why
The grid is mouse-friendly but only partly keyboard- and screen-reader-friendly. Drawers don't trap
focus, and logo tiles announce only a title attribute.

## What
- Arrow-key navigation between day cells; Enter opens the drawer.
- Drawers and the palette trap focus and restore it on close (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`).
- Logo tiles get accessible names ("Apple, reports after close, beat").
- Result colours always have a text or shape cue in addition to colour.
- Check contrast in both themes.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Keyboard-only: reach a company, open its sheet and close it, with focus returning to the tile
- [ ] axe-core finds no serious or critical violations on `/`, an open sheet, `/compass` and `/retrace` (both themes)
- [ ] All text meets WCAG AA contrast

## Test plan
Add `@axe-core/playwright` e2e checks plus a keyboard-navigation e2e test.

## Out of scope
Full screen-reader audio walkthrough.
