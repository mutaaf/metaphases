## Defects, ranked by damage to trust and understanding
1. **P0 · An unknown next session still wipes the history → 0090 (new).** `/api/ticker/SYM` for all 67 companies on `/api/calendar?from=2026-11-19&to=2026-12-01`, re-read 4 s apart: 15 have `history[].session` all `""`, 0 measured moves, `expectedMove: null`, and empty `warnings`. These are DELL, ADSK, INTU, DE, HP, MRVL (date-only overrides), KSS, URBN, AEO (date contests), ASAN, FIVE, PVH, XP, WOOF (blank feed time) and ROAD. Dell's sheet: "Time TBD … Typical move —" (`shots/p__t_DELL.png`). From the sheet's own closes, Dell's day-after moves were +15.8, +32.8, +21.9 and +5.8%, so the after-close test wins 4 of 4 and the typical move is about ±19%. Cause: `worker/index.ts:821`'s non-projected branch, `sessionHabit(nextE.hour = "", nqNextHour, lastE?.hour)`, where `lastE` is gone the day after the last print. This is the generalisation of 0088's lesson.
2. **P1 · "Time TBD" is all a trader gets for Dell, Autodesk and Marvell → 0091 (new).** The overrides correctly don't turn a call time into a session, but the sheet could say "usually after the close" (0086's habit pattern) and show the company's call time from the override note.
3. **P1 · Under peak-week load, sheets lose the date the calendar has → 0092 (new).** Reading 67 sheets 0.3 s apart, ZM, HPE, HPQ, WDAY and NTES came back with "Upcoming date unavailable." (plus history, profile and ratings). The calendar listed ZM, HPE, HPQ and WDAY with their right dates and sessions at the same time. Two minutes later, read 4 s apart, all were complete. My burst caused it, but a dozen people tapping through peak week cause the same Finnhub load (one key, 60 a minute).
4. **Range-dependent estimates, a cold read (0085 lesson, not refiled).** The first read of `11-19→12-01` had `eE: null` for PDD, WSM, BBWI, ROST and GAP on their report day. The next five reads, across five ranges, all had the estimates (PDD 19.213, ROST 1.9138, GAP 0.7855). This is the known cache-only limit of `carryEstimates` on a cold colo, documented in LESSONS (0085). It's worth a ticket if a later critique sees it twice.
5. **Carried, not refiled:** 0065 (the phone's first visit opens behind "How do you invest?", `shots/p__.png`), 0071, 0073, 0074, 0077. The `/api/calendar` window was cold for all 9 days of `11-19→12-01` (`uncheckedDays`) on the first read (0083 behaviour, shown honestly).
