## Defects, ranked by damage to trust and understanding

1. **P0 · Report day stays in the future tense → 0039 (new).** `worker/index.ts:190` picks `next` as the first event dated today or later and ignores its actual. History only arrives the next day via Nasdaq. So for the hours of peak attention, every same-day reporter's sheet shows "NEXT REPORT · Before open · today · EPS EST", The Read says "reports… Wall Street expects… *into the print* it's in an uptrend" (that's post-report price action), the "Write the preview" button invites an AI preview of a finished report, and `/t/PFE` unfurls "PFE reports Tue, Nov 3 before the open · Street expects $0.73". Meanwhile the calendar cell shows the $0.87 beat. Evidence: `p_t_PFE_sheet.png`, `p_t_APO_sheet.png`. The same holds for SHOP, ETN and MPC (`/api/ticker` `next.date = 2026-11-03`).
2. **P0 · Orphaned reports are spreading (0038, sharpened).** Six names now, and WES is confirmed by the company: Western Midstream reports **tomorrow** after the close, and Metaphases says it's 105 days away. FIS will join tonight.
3. **P1 · A missing actual and an off-basis actual → 0040 (new).** ADM's numbers were out at 7 am ET and were still missing 12 hours later, so the Tuesday column treats ADM as not reported. APO's EPS is $2.15 against the company's $2.07, and the revenue actual ($1.374B) isn't Apollo's total revenues ($4.739B).
4. **P0 · Known-wrong dates, sixth critique (0013/0015).** The 9 conflicts are unchanged. At 07:00, Nasdaq matched the company release 5/5 times where Finnhub missed.
5. **P0 · Two beat rates on one screen (0035).** The home page says "73% beat on EPS" and, a scroll later, "39% of those reported so far beat on both lines", with no reconciliation, and both are built on drifted estimates.
6. **P0 · Cloudflare beacon (0036).** `static.cloudflareinsights.com` loads on every page in every run (and `grep -c` on `/` with a browser UA = 1).
