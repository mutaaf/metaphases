## Defects, ranked by damage to trust and understanding

1. **P0 · The ticker sheet overflows sideways on phones** → ticket 0007.
   At 390×844 the dialog's `scrollWidth` is 534 against a `clientWidth` of 390. `.next-card` is 534px wide because the `.nc-stats` three-column grid doesn't shrink. "Typical move" is cut to "TYPIC ±2.9" and every line of *The Read* is clipped at the right edge. This is the product's core surface, for every ticker. The e2e no-horizontal-scroll test only measures `document.documentElement`, so it can't catch this. Evidence: `shots/2026-11-01-phone-2-sheet-AAPL.jpg`, `shots/2026-11-01-phone-3-sheet-GOOGL.jpg`. Bar: no element inside `[role=dialog]` extends past the viewport at 390px.

2. **P0 · Revenue actuals and estimates aren't always on the same basis** → ticket 0008.
   GOOGL shows $111.2B against $132.0B, but the reported figure is $127.6B. In the Oct 26–30 window, 17 of 183 tracked reports show a revenue surprise of ±8% or more. Several are improbable: GLXY −42.9%, EXE −28.6%, HIG −27.6%, GOOGL −15.8%. These rows feed the Retrace revenue beat rate, the season scoreboard (`shared/depth.ts:137`) and the AI context. Separately, non-USD filers show local-currency figures as dollars: KSPI "rev est" is $1.27T and EPS est 1571.76, which look like tenge. Bar: no tracked company shows a revenue surprise its own release contradicts, and non-comparable rows are labelled and excluded from beat rates.

3. **P1 · Projected dates read like confirmed dates** → ticket 0009.
   `/t/GOOGL` says "Tuesday, February 2 · After close · in 93 days", and its share unfurl says "GOOGL reports Tue, Feb 2 after the close". Alphabet hasn't announced a Q4 date. This is Finnhub's projection. A trader who sees an exact date 3 months out assumes it's confirmed. Bar: dates that aren't confirmed are visibly marked as expected, in the sheet, the tiles and the og text.

4. **P1 · The same week has two different counts.** At the top of the calendar, "November earnings · **266** companies report this month" (Most popular filter). Directly below it, "This week · Nov 2–6 · **336** companies report" (every company). A first-time user reads that one week has more reports than the whole month. Bar: both counts use the same filter, or the brief says "336 in all · 1xx on your current filter". (Not filed; tickets are capped at 3. It's the next one to file.)

5. **P1 · Retrace is empty during the busiest results week.** On Nov 1, Retrace reads "No results to grade yet", even though MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META, LLY, XOM and CVX reported in the last 6 days. Scoping to the calendar month throws away the most interesting data at month boundaries. Bar: when the current month has fewer than ~25 graded reports, Retrace shows the trailing 30 days (labelled). Evidence: `shots/2026-11-01-phone-4-retrace.jpg`.

6. **P1 · On a cold edge cache, whole days go missing.** The first `GET /api/calendar?from=2026-10-01&to=2026-10-31` returned 71 events with 8 "Couldn't load" warnings (Oct 21–27). The same request seconds later returned 493 events and no warnings. The UI degrades gracefully, but the first visitor after a cache expiry sees a hollow month. Bar: a cold month request returns ≥ 95% of its days, or the client retries the warned days once.

7. **P1 · "Buy below" is product copy.** The watchlist note field in the sheet (`src/components/TickerSheet.tsx:229`) and the Watchlist column (`src/pages/Watchlist.tsx:28`) are labelled "Buy below". It's the user's own number, but the label puts the word "buy" in our UI, against invariant 4. Suggest "Alert below" or "My price". The "Street view · 63% buy" headline is factual analyst-rating data, but it should read "63% of analysts rate it buy" so it can't be taken as our view.

8. **P2 · On a first phone visit, there's no earnings data above the fold.** The hero arc, headline and CTAs fill all 844px, and the floating "Ask about this week" pill covers half of the "Map my portfolio" CTA (`shots/2026-11-01-phone-1-home.jpg`). In dark mode it covers the first week row (`shots/2026-11-01-phone-6-home-dark.jpg`). For the glancer, "two seconds to scan" means they should see at least one row of logos on first load.

9. **P2 · Copy and labelling nits.** *The Read* compares the estimate with *last quarter* ("$2.02 versus $1.91 last quarter"). For seasonal companies (AAPL's December quarter, retailers), the year-ago quarter is the meaningful comparison. For GOOGL the comparison uses GAAP $2.12, which included a one-off loss. GOOGL's sector label is "MEDIA". The calendar toolbar's "+", image and "AB" buttons are unlabelled glyphs with no visible meaning on phone.

## What's right
- Every date and session I checked against company IR was correct, including AAPL's unusual Monday date.
- The share unfurls are specific and good: "AAPL reports Mon, Nov 2 after the close · Street expects $2.02 EPS. Beat 4 of its last 4 · typical move ±2.9%". `/og/t/AAPL.png` returns a 54 KB PNG.
- Compass's sample portfolio maps cleanly: 7 reports, $16K of $52K riding, week by week, with the ETF look-through visible.
- There are no console errors. Market Mood and the macro strip (Jobs in 5 days, CPI in 9) give real context.
- The sheet's grade explanations ("Keeps a big slice of every sale…") are exactly the plain English a first-time investor needs.
