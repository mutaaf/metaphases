## Defects, ranked by damage to trust and understanding

1. **P0 · A contested session still decides the typical move → 0060 (new).** Calendar rows with `x: differs` on the *same* date: ADI, DE, ROAD, WMG. The sheet keeps Finnhub's session (`sessionHabit`, `shared/reaction.ts:21`; `worker/index.ts:549`) and measures every past move with it. From each sheet's own `chart` closes: ADI's moves become −0.9/−3.9/+2.6/+5.3 (±3.2%, shown ±1.7%), DE's +6.9/−5.2/+11.6 (±7.9%, shown ±1.6%), ROAD's +19.6/+6.9/+11.2/−3.6 (±10.3%, shown ±1.5%). WMG is the reverse: Finnhub's after-close is right there. So the fix is to read the reaction or show "—", not "trust Nasdaq". `/t/DE` og:description reads "report time unconfirmed … typical move ±1.6%", which states the number as fact next to an admission that the session is unknown (`shots/p__t_ADI.png`).
2. **P0 · ADI's own announcement isn't used.** ADI published the date and the 7:00 am time on Oct 22. The sheet says "Treat the date as unconfirmed until the company announces it." Folded into 0060 (override + no disagreement note on overridden rows). The company-date pipeline (0013/0045) is still manual, and in peak week it lags: Medtronic's Oct 30 notice is also missing, harmless only because both feeds agree.
3. **P1 · The first calendar read of the morning has no cross-check → 0061 (new).** At about 07:00 UTC: `unchecked: 10`, **0 of 103** rows with `x`. Ninety seconds later: `unchecked: 2`, 70 rows with `x` (17 `differs`). The client merges `unchecked` (`src/lib/api.ts:33`) and then never shows or acts on it (`src/components/Calendar.tsx:345–352` only handles `warnings` and `pending`). This was item 3 at 11-11 19:00; filed now because it recurred.
4. **Carried:** 0050 now looks met on production (TGT/TJX/WMT `bmo`, INTU `amc` on the calendar and the sheet; PANW `amc` from its override). The builder can verify and close it. Still open: 0053 ("Q3 2027" fiscal labels), 0023 (the invented "WATCHING" state) and INTU's estimate above its guide.
